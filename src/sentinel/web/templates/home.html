How it works

  1. 01

    Connect your mail

    Sign in and link Gmail in one click, or any inbox over IMAP. Sentinel reads — it never sends, replies, or deletes.

  2. 02

    Describe what matters

    Plain English: “my kids’ school,” “anything from my landlord,” “replies to job applications.” No rules, no filters to maintain.

  3. 03

    Get a push when it lands

    An important email arrives, you get a notification on your phone with a one-line summary. Everything else stays quiet.

Most of us drown in email. The handful of messages that matter are buried under newsletters, receipts, and noise. Sentinel watches for them so you don’t have to.
